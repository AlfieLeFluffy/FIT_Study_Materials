## Define
Let $x_{1},\dots,x_{n}$ be a random sample from a variable with a finite moments up to order 4, then skewness is defined as: $$Skew=\frac{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n(x_{i}-\overline{x})^3}{\left( \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n(x_{i}-\overline{x})^2 \right)^ \frac{3}{2}}$$and excess kurtosis as: $$Kurt=\frac{\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n(x_{i}-\overline{x})^4}{\left( \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^n(x_{i}-\overline{x})^2 \right)^ \frac{4}{2}}$$
Note that [[Normal Distribution|normal distribution]] has both skewness and excess kurtosis equal to 0.
