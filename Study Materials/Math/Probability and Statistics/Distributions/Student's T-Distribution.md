## Defintion
Let $Z$ be a [[Random Variable|random variable]] following standard [[normal distribution]] and $Y$ be a [[Random Variable|random variable]] following [[Chi Distribution|chi distribution]]. Let $Z$ and $Y$ be independent, then $$X=\frac{Z}{\left( {\frac{Y}{n}} \right)^{1/2}}$$ follows the t-distribution with $n$ degrees of freedom.
