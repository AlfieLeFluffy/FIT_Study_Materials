## Definition
Let $X_{1}$ be a [[Random Variable|random variable]] with [[Chi Distribution]] with $k_{1}$ degrees of freedom and $X_{2}$ be a random variable with [[Chi Distribution|chi distribution]] with $k_{2}$ degrees of freedom. Let $X_{1}$ and $X_{2}$ be independent. The following test statistic follows the Fisher-Snedecor probability  distribution with $k_{1}$ and $k_{2}$ degrees of freedom respectively.
$$F=\frac{X_{1}/k_{1}}{X_{2}/k_{2}}$$
