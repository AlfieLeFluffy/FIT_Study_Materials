## Definition
A **discrete distribution** that describes the **amount of occurrences** of a random value in a **one time interval**.
$$ P(X = x) = \frac{\alpha^x}{x!}e^(-\alpha)$$
$$F(x) = \sum_{x_{i}\leq x} \frac{\alpha^{x_{i}}}{x!}e^(-\alpha)$$
