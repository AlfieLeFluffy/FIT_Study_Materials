## Definition
Let $X_{1},\dots,X_{n}$ be independent identically distributed samples from a standard [[normal distribution]]. Then $\sum_{i=0}^nX_{i}^2$ has the $\chi^2(n)$ distribution.
If $X \approx \chi^2(n)$ then PDF of $X$ is: $$f(x)=\frac{1}{2^{n/2}\times(n/2)}x^{n/2}e^{-n/2};x>0$$
This corresponds to the Gamma distribution with the parameters $\alpha=\frac{n}{2}$ and $\beta=\frac{1}{2}$.
