A complex number can be though of as a point $P=(x,y)$ in a 2D [[Cartesian Coordinate System|Cartesian space]]. The $x$ coordinate is the real part of the number, while the $y$ coordinate is the imaginary part. Another ways of notation for complex numbers are: $$P=x+yi\ \ \text{ or }\ \ P=x+yj$$$$P=u_{m}\cos(\phi)+ju_{m}\sin(\phi) \text{ where } j= \sqrt{ -1}$$
## Rules
### Euler's Relation
Euler's relation tells us that: $$\exp(j\phi)=\cos(\phi)+j\sin(\phi)$$
Using this relation we can thus write: $$P=u_{m}\cos(\phi)+ju_{m}\sin(\phi)=u_{m}\exp(j\phi)$$ where $u_{m}$ is the **amplitude** and $\phi$ the **phase**.
### Complex Association
A number $P^*=x-yj$ is complexly associated with $P=x+yj$
### Size
Size of complex number $P$ is notated as $|P|$ and is $|P|^2=PP^*=\mathrm{Re}(P)^2+\mathrm{Im}(P)^2$
