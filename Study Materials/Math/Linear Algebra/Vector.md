Vector variable are combination of both **size** and **direction** information within some multidimensional space. This space can for example be the [[Cartesian Coordinate System]]. Within this system a vector is notated as $\vec{A} = A_{1}*\vec{i} + A_{2}*\vec{j} + A_{3}*\vec{k}$, where $\vec{i}, \vec{j}, \vec{k}$ are the unit vectors of the space and $A_{1}, A_{2}, A_{3}$ are the coefficients of the vector. The size of a vector can be calculated as $|\vec{Z}|=A=\sqrt{ A_{1}^2+A_{2}^2+A_{3}^2 }$.
## Operations
Some simple operations with vectors are:
- **Addition**
	- Commutative, associative, distributive
	- $\vec{A}+\vec{B}=(A_{1}+B_{1})*\vec{i}+(A_{2}+B_{2})*\vec{i}+(A_{3}+B_{3})*\vec{k}$
- **Subtraction**
	- Commutative, associative, distributive
	- $\vec{A}-\vec{B}=(A_{1}-B_{1})*\vec{i}+(A_{2}-B_{2})*\vec{i}+(A_{3}-B_{3})*\vec{k}$
- **Scalar Multiplication**
	- Commutative, distributive
	- $\vec{A}*\vec{B}=A_{1}*B_{1}+A_{2}*B_{2}+A_{3}*B_{3} = |\vec{A}||\vec{B}|\cos(\theta)$
- **Vector Multiplication**
	- Anticommutative, distribute (addition), homogenous
	- $\vec{A}\times\vec{B}=\dots$
