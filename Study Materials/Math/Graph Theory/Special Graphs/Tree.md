Trees are a simple [[Graph#Continuity|continuous]] [[Graph|graph]] T that does not contain [[Graph Types|circles/loops]]. Another way of calling graphs without loops is **acyclical**. A **forest** is graph made of non-continuous trees. Tree graphs are also skeletons of graphs. For trees it applies that:
- If it contains more then one vertex there exists a vertex with degree 1.#
- Between two vertexes there exists only one possible path.
