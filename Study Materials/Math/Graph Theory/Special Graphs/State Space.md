State space is a [[Graph|graph]], where each vertex represent different state of a task or a process and edges represent used operators to get to such state. Formally it would be a tuple **(S, O)**, where:
- **S** is a [[Set|set]] of state represented by vertexes.
- **O** is a [[Set|set]] of operators represented by edges.
