A weighted graph is such a [[Graph|graph]] that assigns values to its edges. These values can be thought of costs for moving between two vertexes through the weighted edge.
![[Graphs_Examples_Definition_Weighted.excalidraw.svg]]
## Types
There are several types of weighted graphs, but the most important are:
- **Positively** weighted graphs contain only weights that are positive numbers.
- **Negatively** weighted graphs contain only weights that are negative numbers.
