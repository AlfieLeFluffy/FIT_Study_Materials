There are several commonly known types of [[Graph|graphs]] and sub-graphs that are:
- **Path** (P<sub>n</sub>) is a graph of length **n>=0** with **n+1** different vertexes connected in a row by **n** edges. 
	![[Graphs_Examples_Definition_Path.excalidraw.svg]]
- **Circle** (C<sub>n</sub>) is a graph of length **n** with **n>=3** different vertexes connected into one cycle by **n** edges. 
	![[Graphs_Examples_Definition_Circle.excalidraw.svg]]
- **Full Graph** (K<sub>n</sub>) for **n>=1** is a graph of **n** vertexes and edges that connect each vertex to each other vertex with **n<sup>2</sup>** edges.
	![[Graphs_Examples_Definition_Full_Graph.excalidraw.svg]]
- **Full Bipartite Graph** (K<sub>n,m</sub>) for **n>=1** and **m>=1** is a graph of **n+m** vertexes in two groups, where each vertex in one group is connected to every vertex in another graph, creating tuples **(n,m)**. 
	![[Graphs_Examples_Definition_Full_Bipartite_Graph.excalidraw.svg]]
- **Star** (K<sub>1,n</sub>) is a special case of the full bipartite graph with **n>=1** edges, where one part has only one vertex and the other the rest of vertexes.
	![[Graphs_Examples_Definition_Star.excalidraw.svg]]
