Task is a tuple **(S<sub>0</sub>, G)**, **S<sup>0</sup> ∈ S** and **G ⊂ S**, where:
- **S** is task's [[State Space|state space]].
- **S<sub>0</sub>** is the initial/starting state.
- **G** is a [[Set|set]] of all terminal/target/ending states.
