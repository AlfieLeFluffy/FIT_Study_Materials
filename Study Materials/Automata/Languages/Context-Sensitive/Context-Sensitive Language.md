A [[Formal Language|languages]] in the [[Chomsky Hierarchy]] that are generated by [[Context-Sensitive Grammar]] and accepted by [[Linearly Limited Automata]]. Importantly each context-sensitive language is a [[Recursively Enumerable Language|recursive]], but not all recursive languages are context-sensitive.
## Properties
- Closed to operations $\cup$, $\cap$, $*$ and complement.
- The membership problem can be decided (recursion).
- Inclusion of languages cannot be decided.
- The empty problem cannot be decided.
