Multimedia is a field of study/industry that specializes in working with audio, images, animations, videos and many other medias and their combined use.
## Use Cases
There are many common day use cases for multimedia, such as:
- *Entertainment* industry (games, digital screening, music, photography, television)
- *Telecommunication* (telephones, web pages, social media)
- *Publishing* (journalistic, online storage, records keeping)
- *Science* (hyperspectral imaging, plenoptical representation)
- *Digitalization* of cultural heritage
- *Medicine* (sharing of medical images, ultrasound [[video]])
- *Education* (online course)
- *[[Video]] security* (camera systems)
## Related Fields
- [[Signal]] processing (A/D translation, sampling theorem)
- [[Sound]]/Speech processing (speech compression)
- [[Image]] processing (filling in gaps, removing artefacts)
- Data [[Compression|compression]] (all multimedia formats)
- Computer graphics (colour models, rendering)
- Computer vision (inteligent [[Image]] search)
- Cryptography (digital rights management)
- Math ([[Vector|vector]] space)
