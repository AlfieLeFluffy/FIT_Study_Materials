An image is a visual phenomenon perceived by a human.  
## Characteristics
Some basic characteristics of images as related to computers are:
- An image can be captured by a camera, displayed on a monitor or a dataprojektor, edited on a computer and printed through some kind of a printer.
- An image is usually in a [[Raster Graphics|raster graphics]] format, represented as a grid of pixels with its height and width predefined.
- The pixels in an image usually follow some [[Colour|colour]] model.
- Low-level view: pixel format, under-sampling of [[Colour|colours]]
- High-level view: image rendering (showing) from a format, such as PNG, JPEG, etc.
