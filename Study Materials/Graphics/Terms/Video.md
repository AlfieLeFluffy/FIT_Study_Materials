A video is an illusion of continuous motion, created by an arrangement of static [[Image|images]].
## Characteristics
Some basic characteristics of video as related to computers are:
- Video and [[Sound|audio]] are usually stored together in a multimedia container. 
- There exist multimedia frameworks that work with video.
- Low-level view: [[Image|images]], interpretation, frequency
- High-level view: rendering (playing) of video in a format, such as MP4, RAW, etc.
