Most images today are captured through exposure of digital sensor through a lens assembly, shutter, etc. Common image capturing sensor architectures are:
- [[Charge-Coupled Device Sensor|CCD sensor]]
- [[Complementary Metal-Oxide-Semiconductor Sensor|CMOS sensor]]
Resulting images also depend on other relating [[Camera Settings|characteristics]] of the whole capturing assembly (camera). [[Coloured Image Capturing|Capturing coloured images]] also requires more complex assembly.
