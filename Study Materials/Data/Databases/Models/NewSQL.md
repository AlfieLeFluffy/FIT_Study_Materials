NewSQL databases provide [[NoSQL]] features on [[ACID]]-complaint [[Structured Query Language|SQL]] databases. 
## Properties
- This means that these systems are high-throughput and high-available with transactional consistency. 
- They offer strong consistency by sacrificing some availability by using consensus protocols such as Paxos and Raft and by forcing durability or synchronous replications as in MemSQL. 
- They support [[Structured Query Language|SQL]] interfaces and backwards compatibility to [[Relation Data Model]].
Examples of such databases are ClustrixDB, NuoDB, CockroachDB, MemSQL, VoltDB, Percona TokuDB, ActorDB
