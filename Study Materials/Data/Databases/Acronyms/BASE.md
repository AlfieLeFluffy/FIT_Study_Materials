## BASE
Acronym for [[NoSQL]] databases properties:
- **Basically Available**: An application works basically all the time.
- **Soft-state**: It does not have to be consistent all the time.
- **Eventually consistent**: Eventually it will be in some known state.
Inconsistency is usually solved during reading through versioning and nonvalid cache and during writing through distribution of changes or data replication.
