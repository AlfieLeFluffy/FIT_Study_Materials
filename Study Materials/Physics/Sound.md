Any sound is just a mechanical vibration that travels through the air (or other material), which can be perceived by a human.
## Characteristics
Some basic characteristics of sound as related to computers are:
- Most commonly represented as a digital sound signal.
- Humans commonly perceive sounds between 20 and 20 000 Hz
- A/D and D/A [[Convertor|convertors]]
- Nyquist's frequency
- Low-level view: sound cards, audio sampling
- High-level view: playback of formats such as MP3, AC3, AAC
