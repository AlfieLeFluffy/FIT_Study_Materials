- **Synchronous** - Together with the data a clock signal ([[Clock Signal]]) is sent. The signal can be interpreted in several ways depending on the interface (data is valid on the rising/stable/falling edge).
- **Asynchronous** - There is not clock signal ([[Clock Signal]]) sent with the data. The receiver and transmitter generate it themselves and there must be one setup (agreed upon/configured) [[Baud Rate]].
